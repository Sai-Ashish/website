This semantic layer provides the foundation for our NL2SQL work. I co-led OraPlan-SQL to first place in the 2025 Archer NL2SQL Challenge and SOMA-SQL to #1 on Spider 2.0 Lite in April 2026, two competitive enterprise NL2SQL benchmarks.
